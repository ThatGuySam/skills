# Previous Versus Updated Agent Verify

Date: October 31, 2026. Verdict: **improved on tested cases**, narrowly. The observed gain was one more verification artifact usable without material repair, not broad reliability or measured user time savings.

## Method

The actual pre-update runtime package was preserved as the baseline and the governing outer verification workflow. Both complete runtime packages were frozen. Their entrypoint, references and optional checker were compared; distribution smoke fixtures were not the treatment. Independent designers used the previous workflow and source records, with instructions to exclude the updated verifier and its diff. Rubrics were frozen before each stage.

Four task units received two fresh-context repetitions per package: sixteen verifier runs and eight paired comparisons. The initial three assessment/execution tasks were frozen together and produced six ties. A separately frozen experiment-design extension was added after those ties; it was not a preselected four-task confirmation.

Generation requested OpenAI GPT-6.1 Terra, high reasoning, with accepted launches. Exact native executed-model and reasoning telemetry, tokens and cost were unavailable. Reviewers and the adjudicator used inherited coordinator settings with exact native metadata unavailable. No model escalation occurred.

No critical fault was established.

In the other design pair, one reviewer preferred the old protocol's closer coverage of a known problem; another called it a tie. Adjudication retained the narrower-coverage argument but concluded that the task and rubric did not establish its priority over a broader useful test. All original grades and the contrary finding were preserved.

Both packages correctly rejected a serialization-only change as evidence of better research. Their four submitted comparison programs independently replayed with exit zero and exactly reproduced the saved results. That replay establishes program/result consistency, not original native tool execution.

## Evidence Limits

- The source material and complete worker artifacts remain private. They are not included in this public package, so this summary is not independently replayable from this repository.
- Some tasks were exposed development diagnostics. The representation control was transparently constructed around one real report. The design extension was adaptively selected after initial ties.
- Grading used the frozen previous workflow and source-grounded independent rubrics, not the updated skill as its own evaluator. Both reviewer orders passed focused constructed calibration controls. Those controls are not human preference calibration.
- Package identities were withheld, but citations inside outputs could reveal package traits. Isolation was instruction-based rather than OS-enforced.
- Nested delegation was disabled because independent QA was provided by the outer comparison. Automatic discovery and unrestricted autonomous orchestration were not tested.
- No implemented user workflow, prospective research experiment, cross-model transfer, escalation accuracy or real-world savings was established.

The smallest next check is an operator replay of the proposed grader setup, followed by additional independently selected verification-design tasks. The current evidence supports one local protocol-completeness gain and seven paired ties.
