# Method sources

These links support the method. They are not prerequisites for ordinary use. The full research and evidence limits live in the [research memo](https://github.com/ThatGuySam/skills/blob/main/apps/docs/src/content/docs/research/andrej-explain-2026-10-01.md).

- [Karpathy's originating post](https://x.com/karpathy/status/2105819303471976479): controlled writing, diagrams, HTML, and bespoke video to help people understand model output. The permalink was matched through a mirror in the earlier research thread; direct X retrieval was blocked. Do not treat its format sequence as comparative evidence.
- [2025 year in review](https://karpathy.bearblog.dev/year-in-review-2025/): disposable software and interfaces beyond chat.
- [Sequoia Ascent 2026](https://karpathy.bearblog.dev/sequoia-ascent-2026/): preserve understanding while delegating work. The page identifies its AI-assisted summary/transcript provenance.
- [microgpt](https://karpathy.github.io/2026/02/12/microgpt/): small concrete implementations as explanation.
- [ASD-STE100](https://www.asd-ste100.org/): authoritative specification for strict controlled-language claims. The skill's default is an adaptation, not certified compliance.
- [Bret Victor, Explorable Explanations](https://worrydream.com/ExplorableExplanations/): interaction that lets readers inspect assumptions and consequences.
- [Mayer and Moreno, Nine Ways to Reduce Cognitive Load in Multimedia Learning](https://doi.org/10.1207/S15326985EP3801_6): segment explanations, coordinate words and visuals, and remove distractions within the paper's conditions.
- [Andy Matuschak, Why books don't work](https://andymatuschak.org/books/): distinguish exposure from durable understanding.
- [Olah and Carter, Research Debt](https://distill.pub/2017/research-debt/): improve the conceptual explanation before polishing its presentation.
- [Agent Skills best practices](https://agentskills.io/skill-creation/best-practices) and [evaluation](https://agentskills.io/skill-creation/evaluating-skills): focused workflows, conditional resources, representative execution tests.
- [Matt Pocock, writing-for-agents](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills/blob/main/skills/productivity/writing-for-agents/SKILL.md): explicit context pointers and completion criteria. [teach](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills/blob/main/skills/productivity/teach/SKILL.md) is a useful comparison for deeper learning sessions.
- [Zach Prompting](https://github.com/ThatGuySam/skills/blob/main/skills/zach-prompting/SKILL.md): outcome, preserved contract, conditional tool guidance, and observed verification. Used to author this skill; not a runtime dependency.

This independently authored skill is inspired by Andrej Karpathy's writing. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by him. Linked material retains its original rights; the specification and other source texts are not bundled here.
