# Search Off the Record findings on AI content

Paraphrased from Google's published transcripts of the Search Off the Record podcast. Remarks by Search Relations staff are commentary, not formal policy; guest remarks carry less weight still. Transcripts were published by Google and have not been verified against audio. Reviewed 2026-10-07 across 111 available transcripts; the December 2025 episode "Thoughts on SEO & SEO for AI, part 1" had no transcript available and is not covered.

Use these to explain why the formal rules exist and how Googlers think about AI-assisted writing. When a podcast remark and current documentation differ, follow the documentation.

## Current direction (2023–2026)

**Don't make machine-shaped content.** Danny Sullivan said Google does not want people splitting content into bite-sized chunks for LLMs or keeping one version for LLMs and another for people. Tactics tuned to a current system may stop working as systems improve toward rewarding content written for humans. For a local business, he suggested effort goes further on happy customers and reviews than on restructuring pages for LLMs. — [SEO, AIO, GEO, your site, & third-party support to optimize for LLMs](https://search-off-the-record.libsyn.com/seo-aio-geo-your-site-third-party-support-to-optimize-for-llms), 2026-01-08.

**Generic AI sites can lose indexing.** John Mueller said that when Google's systems have serious concerns about a site's overall quality, they crawl and index less. He gave the example of a mostly AI-generated site that once worked but where readers can tell nothing unique is offered, and said not all AI-generated content is bad. He framed the fix as stepping back to assess quality rather than chasing a technical cause. — [How to read the Indexing Report](https://search-off-the-record.libsyn.com/how-to-read-the-indexing-report), 2026-07-16.

**Authentic content is the durable bet.** Discussing ranking systems, John Mueller said authentic content made for a known audience is the way forward, and that AI content will have useful places while the foundation remains pointing people to authentic information. Danny Sullivan said a ranking-system update should not change what a creator does if they are already making helpful content. — [Let's talk ranking updates](https://search-off-the-record.libsyn.com/help-the-algorithm-has-been-updated), 2023-08-22.

**Use AI to start, then rewrite.** Martin Splitt described using generative AI to get past the blank page, then rejecting and reworking the draft into what he meant to say. He hoped creators would learn to use AI well rather than flood the web with it. — [2023 year in review](https://search-off-the-record.libsyn.com/2023-year-in-review), 2023-12-21.

**The problem is how it's used.** Gary Illyes said generative tools are not inherently bad and can help with structure and wording under deadline; John Mueller mentioned brainstorming. — [Which aspect of my site should I focus on?](https://search-off-the-record.libsyn.com/which-aspect-of-my-site-should-i-focus-on), 2023-11-21.

**Always fact-check.** In an episode testing Gemini-written social posts, Gary Illyes said his main concern with generative interfaces is factuality and that output always needs fact-checking. He noted grounding against search results does not guarantee correctness. — [Gemini for Social Posts: Game Edition](https://search-off-the-record.libsyn.com/gemini-for-social-posts-game-edition), 2024-04-25.

## Practitioner perspective (guest)

**Edited AI can work; churn does not.** Guest Jennifer Slegg said she had seen good results when a skilled copywriter reworked AI drafts, but that mass-publishing thousands of generated articles is not the way to go. — [From journalism to SEO with Jennifer Slegg](https://search-off-the-record.libsyn.com/from-journalism-to-seo-with-jennifer-slegg), 2022-11-30.

## Superseded framing

**2021: human supervision as the line.** Gary Illyes said Google's stance then was that machine-generated content without human supervision was unwanted in Search, while reviewed content was fine. John Mueller raised the risk of fluent but wrong output. — [The future of SEO](https://search-off-the-record.libsyn.com/the-future-of-seo), 2021-11-11.

This predates the February 2023 guidance, which judges content by value and purpose rather than by production method. Keep the fact-checking concern; do not present "a human looked at it" as sufficient.

## Patterns

- Every current source treats value to readers as the test and scale without value as the risk. This is consistent across formal docs and commentary.
- Googlers describe AI as a writing aid (drafts, structure, brainstorming) and consistently pair it with human rewriting and fact-checking.
- No episode reviewed gives a ranking benefit for AI disclosure or a method for detecting AI text. Inference: avoid claiming either.
- Coverage is uneven. Most remarks are brief asides in episodes about other topics, and no full episode on AI-written content was available.
