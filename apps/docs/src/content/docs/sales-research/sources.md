The skill chooses sources based on the sale. Its default prior starts with Blair
Enns, David C. Baker, Jonathan Stark, Peter Block, Mahan Khalsa and Randy Illig,
Brennan Dunn, Jason Liu, Brent Ozar, Paul Swail, Karl Sakas, David A. Fields, and
Philip Morgan. Comparable experience and stronger evidence outrank that order.

## Evidence and attribution

The skill prefers primary work, comparable firsthand cases, and relevant
technical or procurement evidence. It labels inference and self-reported results,
and avoids generic roundups and unsupported advice. If sources conflict, current
buyer facts and the user's constraints take precedence over a general framework.

This distribution contains original instructions and links, not third-party
books, courses, article bodies, recordings, or transcripts. It does not include
private client records. Named practitioners and publishers do not endorse it.
