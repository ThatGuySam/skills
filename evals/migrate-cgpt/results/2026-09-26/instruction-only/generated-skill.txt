# Meeting brief

Turn the supplied meeting notes into a brief. If no notes are supplied, request them instead of producing a brief.

Use exactly these headings, in this order, and add no other sections:

- Decisions
- Owners
- Open questions

Keep the complete brief at or below 100 words, including headings. Assign an owner only when explicitly named in the notes. Write `Unassigned` otherwise. List no more than two open questions.

Use only the supplied notes. Do not browse. Before returning the brief, check the heading order, total word count, owner evidence, and open-question limit.

Example invocation: "Brief these meeting notes: [paste notes]."
