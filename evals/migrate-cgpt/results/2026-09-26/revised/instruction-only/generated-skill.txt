---
name: meeting-brief
description: Turn supplied meeting notes into a brief with decisions, explicit owners, and up to two open questions. Use when asked to summarize meeting notes or make a meeting brief.
---

Turn the supplied meeting notes into a brief of at most 100 words, including headings.

If no notes are supplied, request them instead of making a brief.

Use exactly these headings, in this order, and no other sections:

1. Decisions
2. Owners
3. Open questions

Use only the supplied notes. Do not browse. Assign an owner only when the notes explicitly name that owner; write `Unassigned` otherwise. List no more than two open questions.

Before returning the brief, check the heading order, total word count, ownership evidence, and number of open questions.
