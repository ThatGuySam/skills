This simulated capture points to the build competing with typing and app switching for CPU time. The Mac stays 96% busy; the two clang compiler processes use roughly 7.5 of its 8 logical CPUs across the valid samples. That makes CPU contention a likely contributor, though it does not prove the cause.

Memory looks less concerning: swap and compression do not grow. Current disk traffic is modest, around 0.6–0.7 MB/s; the larger first reading is a historical average. Heat and network activity were not measured.

The most useful next step is to pause the build through its controls and check whether typing and app switching recover.
