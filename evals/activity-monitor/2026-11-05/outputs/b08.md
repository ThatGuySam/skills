This simulated capture points to memory swapping as a likely contributor to the tab freezes. CPU has plenty of room: 87–88% is idle, and Safari uses about a quarter of one core.

Safari holds about 19 GB of memory. During each five-second interval, roughly 938 MiB moves into RAM from swap and another 938 MiB moves out. Swap stays at 9 GB, but that steady total hides substantial movement during the freezes.

Save your work, close a few unneeded large tabs, then try switching tabs again. Improvement would help confirm the memory explanation. Disk latency, heat, and network were not measured.
