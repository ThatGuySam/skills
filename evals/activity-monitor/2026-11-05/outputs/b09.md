In this simulated capture, CPU has plenty of headroom: 86% idle during the stutters. Safari is not an obvious culprit, so there is no clear reason to close it.

Pageouts do rise during these 10 seconds: 200,000 pages, about 3.1 GiB. That is current activity, not merely an old total, but it does not prove you need more RAM. Swap and compression stay unchanged, with substantial memory available.

The backup coincides with 200–210 MB/s of disk traffic. Storage contention is plausible, but throughput alone does not prove a bottleneck; disk latency is missing.

The most useful next step is to briefly pause the backup through its controls and repeat app switching to see whether the stutters stop.
