This simulated capture points to memory swapping as a likely contributor to the browser freezes. CPU has plenty of room: 87–88% is idle.

Safari holds about 19 GB of the Mac’s 32 GB RAM. During each five-second interval, nearly 1 GiB moves into swap and another 1 GiB moves out. Those repeated rows represent ongoing traffic, even though total swap usage stays at 9 GB.

Save your work, close a few unneeded large tabs, then try switching tabs again to see whether freezes ease. Disk latency, heat, and network were not captured, so their contribution remains unknown.
