This practice capture is synthetic, so it does not establish what is happening on your real Mac.

Within the example, CPU has plenty of headroom: 86–87% is idle. Memory also looks comfortable, with 65% reported free and no increase in compression or swapping. Pageouts rise during the capture, but that alone does not prove you need more RAM. There is no clear reason here to close Safari.

Disk traffic jumps from about 6 MB/s to 200–210 MB/s while the backup writes show waits. Backup-related disk activity is a plausible cause of the brief stutters, though the capture lacks disk latency and a comparison without the backup.

The most useful next step is to compare app switching after the backup finishes, or briefly pause it. If the stutters disappear, that strengthens the case for scheduling backups during quieter periods.
