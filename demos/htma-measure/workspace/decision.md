# Decision: should we keep paying for the on-call paging tool? (synthetic demo input)

This scenario is invented for a skill demo. The team and numbers are not real.

- Team: 6 engineers share a weekly on-call rotation for one web app.
- Current paging tool costs $3,600 per year. Renewal is due in 30 days.
- Alternative: route alerts to a shared chat channel for free and drop the tool.
- Last quarter there were 14 production alerts; 3 were real outages, the rest were noise.
- One outage last year was noticed late (about 40 minutes) because nobody saw a chat message at night.
- Rough revenue during business hours is about $2,000 per hour; nights are much lower.
- We would cancel if the expected yearly cost of slower response is clearly below $3,600.
